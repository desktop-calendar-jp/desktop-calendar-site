Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

About the Application

Desktop Calendar is a personal Windows desktop calendar application.

Google Calendar Data

The application requests read-only access to Google Calendar to display calendar events.

The application does not create, modify, or delete Google Calendar events.

Data Storage and Sharing

Google Calendar information is used to display events within the application. Authentication credentials may be stored locally on the user's computer to maintain access.

The application does not sell Google Calendar data or share it with advertisers.

Access Revocation

Users can revoke the application's access through their Google Account security settings.
